About the Community

One community to level up as a builder.

The AWS Student Builder Group at SB Jain Institute of Technology is a student-led technology community focused on cloud computing, AWS architecture, DevOps, AI, and modern software engineering.

No gatekeeping. No passive listening. Just real hands-on learning from people who build in the cloud, practical workshops on scalable architecture, and a passionate student network that supports you throughout your career.

01
Learn Cloud & AI Workshops and training sessions designed specifically for students starting or accelerating their AWS journey.
02
Build Real Solutions Turn conceptual ideas into live, production-grade cloud architectures, serverless backends, and AI applications.
03
Connect with Leaders Engage directly with AWS Community Builders, industry architects, and peers across Central India.
WHY JOIN SB JAIN AWS SBG
  • Hands-on AWS Workshops: EC2, Lambda, S3, ECS, and Bedrock generative AI sessions.
  • Certifications & Guidance: Mentorship for AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and Solutions Architect.
  • Hackathons & Challenges: Team up, build cloud apps, and win official AWS swag kits and prizes.
  • Direct Industry Access: Connect with AWS User Groups, guest mentors, and alumni working in top tech firms.
